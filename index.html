Where Does Food Waste Originate?

Food waste is split across three key sectors: households, retail grocery shops, and the food-service industry. Explore their relative contribution and per-person numbers.

Household Waste

84.3 kg / person

Makes up 61.2% of all wasted food. Everyday decisions like cooking too much, improper storage, and expired groceries in private kitchens create the single largest volume.

Food Service

27.4 kg / person

Accounts for 25.7% of the total. Originates from restaurants, staff canteens, hotels, and event catering, primarily through plate waste and unserved prepared food.

Retail & Supermarkets

15.1 kg / person

Represents 13.1% of waste. Modern commercial inventory systems keep retail waste lower than homes, though cosmetic sorting of fresh produce remains an issue.
